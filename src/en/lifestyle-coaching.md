tab_intro:
  section1_title: What is lifestyle Coaching?
  section1_subtitle: Personal Growth, Body Awareness, Resilience & Balance
  section1_text: >-
    Lifestyle coaching helps you become more aware of your daily choices and
    habits. It goes beyond physical health, also considering your **mental** and
    **emotional** wellbeing, your environment, and the way you navigate life's
    challenges.


    By gaining insight into your behaviour, motivation, and patterns, you discover what works for you and how to make lasting changes step by step. This includes exploring the unique interplay between your **body**, **hormones**, **behaviour**, and the **world** around you. The roles, expectations, and challenges you experience as a woman can also influence your wellbeing and the choices you make.


    In this way, you can develop habits that work for you, better understand what you need, and build a lifestyle that sustainably supports **your body and wellbeing.**
  section2_title: Why now?
  section2_subtitle: Your health deserves attention.
  section2_text: >-
    Women's health is receiving more attention than ever. In the **past**,
    women's symptoms and experiences were not always taken seriously or fully
    understood. **Today**, we are gaining a deeper understanding of the
    complexity of the female body and how hormones, lifestyle, and our
    environment influence how we feel and function. Yet there is still much to
    discover.


    But you don't have to wait for science to have all the answers. You can start **listening** to your body, **exploring** what you experience, and taking your own needs **seriously** today.


    By developing a better understanding of your body, behaviour, thoughts, and environment, you can discover what works for you and make conscious choices that support your wellbeing. This is how you build greater **calm**, **confidence**, and **balance** in your daily life.
