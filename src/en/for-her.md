  section1_subtitle: Body Awareness, Strength and Hormonal Health
  section1_text: >-
    Coaching for Her combines **personal training**, **lifestyle coaching** and
    knowledge of the female body. Your health is influenced by many factors,
    including movement, nutrition, sleep, stress and hormonal changes.


    Our approach looks at **the whole picture** and what you need. Whether you want to build strength, find more balance in your lifestyle or better understand what hormonal changes mean for you, your coaching is tailored to your needs, goals and stage of life.


    You’ll gain a better **understanding of your body** and **practical tools** to make informed choices. This helps you build a strong foundation for your health, with greater knowledge, confidence and self-awareness.
  section2_title: Why Now?
  section2_subtitle: Build a strong body at every stage of life.
  section2_text: >-
    You don’t have to wait until you experience symptoms or notice changes to
    start **prioritising your health**. Whether you’re in your thirties and
    looking ahead, beginning to notice changes, or already in perimenopause or
    menopause, investing in your strength, health and lifestyle is valuable.
    **At every age**, you can take steps to support your body in the long term.


    The years **before perimenopause** offer a valuable opportunity to build muscle mass, strength, strong bones and healthy habits. Think of it as laying a solid foundation: the more strength and muscle you build, the more you have to draw on and maintain later in life. **Strength training** plays an important role, but good **nutrition** and sufficient **sleep** are essential too. They help your body recover, adapt to training and stay strong.


    **During perimenopause** and **menopause**, hormonal changes can affect your sleep, energy levels, recovery and body composition, among other things. Every woman’s experience and needs are different. This is why it’s valuable to understand how training, nutrition and recovery influence one another, and how to adapt them to support your individual needs.


    You can’t prevent hormonal changes, but there’s a lot you can do to support your body. As **knowledge about women’s health continues to grow**, there is real value in understanding and applying these insights. By consciously working on your strength, nutrition, sleep and recovery, you’re investing in your health and resilience. Not by fighting your body, but by **learning to work with it**. This helps you build a strong foundation to support you through every stage of life.
