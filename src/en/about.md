  greeting: Hi, I'm Feli Aarsman.
  subtitle: Explore, experience, inspire, and trust
  top_text: >-
    I moved to Wageningen to study **Ecology** at university, where my
    fascination with the **connection between humans and nature** began to grow.
    Just like ecosystems, our bodies depend on a complex interplay of internal
    and external factors. Our health is shaped by biological, psychological and
    social factors that constantly influence one another. This forms the
    foundation of my approach: **we are not machines, but human beings**, each
    with a unique biology, individual needs and a body that is constantly
    changing.


    Over the years, my interests gradually shifted from the academic world towards **sport, movement and a healthy lifestyle**. I found my own balance through adventures in the mountains and on whitewater rivers, alongside my work as a fitness coach. There, I discovered the importance of body awareness, posture, muscle control and breathing. It led me to a question that remains central to my work today: **how can we function at our best by working with our bodies, rather than against them?**
  profile_image: /img/Feli_profile.jpg
  bottom_text: >-
    That question inspired me to **guide women** toward greater physical
    strength, self-confidence, and a deeper understanding of their own bodies.
    In the summer, I share my love for the outdoors as a **whitewater kayak
    instructor** in the mountains of **Slovenia.** The rest of the year, I guide
    women as a lifestyle coach and personal trainer. I help them better
    understand their bodies and build a strong foundation through focused
    attention on **training, lifestyle, and hormonal health.**


    My choice to focus specifically on women stems from the belief that **women's health and female physiology deserve more attention**. With over eight years of experience as an instructor and coach, I have seen how much can change when women gain confidence in their bodies, **discover their strength**, and **dare to take up the space they deserve**. I want to help women better understand their bodies, make their own choices, and **confidently claim their place, both in sports and in life.** Because every woman deserves to feel strong, to be heard, and to chart her own course.
